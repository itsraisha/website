Profile

I like cybersecurity most when the work becomes investigative: a scan becomes a pattern, a login trail becomes a timeline, and scattered logs start telling one story. My strongest project work so far has been around honeypots, SIEM dashboards and open-source detection pipelines.

I’m targeting SOC analyst and security operations roles where I can keep building the habits that matter: asking better questions, validating evidence, writing clear notes, tuning noisy alerts and staying calm when something looks strange.
